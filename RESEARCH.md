# Public-site research inventory

Reviewed on 2026-08-14. The reconstruction was derived from public material only.

## Product and platform pages

- `/` : product positioning, connect -> score -> normalize -> report/ticket workflow; Threats, Risks, Incidents, Compliance, team, RBAC, notifications and reporting.
- `/platform` : TRIC operating model.
- `/platform/threats` : six Threat Objectives and Threat Profile rationale.
- `/platform/risks` : intelligent risk register, RAMP scoring, normalization, remediation and SLA velocity.
- `/platform/incidents` : CIRP severity rubric, unified record, SEV-1 to SEV-5, response timeline and linked-risk lessons learned.
- `/platform/compliance` : RAMP, CIRP and CISP program documentation, policy as a service and governance reports.
- Prompt Governance, Board Reporting, Portfolio Management, Risk-Based Vulnerability Management and Threat Intelligence pages : governed procedure changes and approval, board deck structure, multi-company rollups, normalized risk intake and threat-intelligence-to-risk workflows.

## Solution, company and legal surfaces

Reviewed public solution pages for Digital Risk Governance, Enterprise Risk Management, GRC, Integrated Risk Management and Policy as a Service, plus About, Careers, Contact, Privacy, Terms, Vulnerability Disclosure, Blog and Podcast content indexes. A public bug-bounty scope page and blog and tag surfaces were also identified in search. Marketing content was used to clarify intent, not copied into the app.

## Public docs and release-note behavior used

- Risk and incident public API field shapes, status enums and associations.
- Threat Profile proposal and draft concepts.
- Risk and incident flow analytics.
- Jira, Linear, ServiceNow, CrowdStrike, Wiz, HackerOne, WatchTowr, GreyMatter, Slack and Teams public integration descriptions.
- Seven public RBAC role names.
- Release-note features such as configurable CSV export, tags and filter views, URL-shareable filters, scoring suggestions with reasoning and context, notifications, live governance reporting and integration automation.

## Product imagery understood and reproduced structurally

Public screenshots showed a dark navy left rail, a dense white Risk Register, Remediation Agility visualization, filter, export and add actions, row scoring pills, an Incident Register with a right-side detail drawer, incident detail fields and activity, severity and threat-objective cards, ticket buttons, linked-risk workflow, Compliance governance and program-documentation cards, and a four-part board reporting sequence covering executive summary, Threat Profile, risks and remediation agility, and incident timeline.

The code intentionally recreates these layout and workflow patterns with original CSS and demo data. It does not redistribute third-party logos, screenshots or other proprietary image assets.

## Fidelity limitations

The authenticated app URL was not accessible to the public browsing tool because it returned HTTP 403, and private backend behavior is therefore not observable. Exact prompts, internal RAMP and CIRP math, integration schemas, customer-specific configuration, auth provider and generated-report design are approximated where public material does not disclose them.
